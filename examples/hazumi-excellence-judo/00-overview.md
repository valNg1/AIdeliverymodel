# France Judo — Excellence Judo LMS

> **Pedagogical example — fictionalized case.**
> This example is inspired by a judo learning product and is used solely to demonstrate the AI Delivery Model. It does not describe an actual France Judo initiative.

A worked example that walks one business need through the AI Delivery Model, from need to the end of the **first delivery loop** — no further.

## What you will learn

How the framework connects a business need to an explicit **Intent**, an **AI-native Discovery**, an **AI Impact Canvas**, and a **first delivery loop** that produces working software *and* a **Living Specification** — applying the practice [Intent-Based Development](../../03-delivery/intent-based-development.md):

> **Intent before Build. Specification through Delivery.**

## The fictional business context

"France Judo" (fictional) owns a significant body of pedagogical knowledge associated with its "Excellence Judo" program. Today that knowledge is fragmented across documents, videos, expert know-how, training materials, and informal practices. It is hard to maintain and hard for practitioners to consume as a coherent learning path.

The organization wants an internal, custom-built **LMS** capable of:

- structuring the knowledge base;
- organizing pedagogical content;
- supporting learner progression;
- maintaining editorial ownership;
- making knowledge reusable;
- enabling future evolution of the learning experience.

This is a good candidate for **Make** rather than **Buy**: the knowledge base is the differentiating asset, and the organization wants to own its structure and evolution (see [from-buy-to-make](../../00-vision/from-buy-to-make.md)).
