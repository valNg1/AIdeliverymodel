# Portfolio Governance

Products are funded and managed as a portfolio, not as disconnected projects. This is a first version.

## Principle: Product over Project

A project ends and disperses accountability. A product persists, with an owner and a Run plan. Governance follows products across their whole life.

## What portfolio governance decides

- **Build vs. buy** per capability — see [from-buy-to-make](../00-vision/from-buy-to-make.md).
- **Funding** durable products, including their Run.
- **Ownership** — each product has a single accountable owner.
- **Prioritization** based on business value.
- **Health** — is each product supervised and maintained? See [Product Operations](../05-product-operations/overview.md).

## Lightweight by design

Governance must be fast enough that teams prefer it to routing around it — see [shadow-it-prevention](shadow-it-prevention.md). Favor clear ownership and good Discovery over heavy approval gates.

## Inputs

Portfolio decisions draw on the [AI Impact Canvas](../02-ai-discovery/ai-impact-canvas.md) (value, cost, risk, Run impact) and the [ownership matrix](../templates/ownership-matrix.md).
