# Retrospectives

Retrospectives turn a completed delivery (and an ongoing Run) into captured lessons. This is a first version.

## Purpose

Make learning explicit and reusable, so the same mistakes are not repeated and good practices spread.

## When to run them

- After a delivery increment.
- Periodically during the Run, informed by the [observability matrix](../05-product-operations/observability-matrix.md).
- After a significant incident or decision.

## A simple structure

1. **What happened** — facts, not blame.
2. **What worked** — keep and promote to the [reference model](reference-model.md).
3. **What did not** — and why.
4. **What we will change** — owned actions with dates.
5. **What we learned about the Run** — feed the [research backlog](../05-product-operations/research-backlog.md).

## Outputs

- Updated references and captured knowledge.
- Owned improvement actions.
- Inputs to future [Discovery](../02-ai-discovery/overview.md).
