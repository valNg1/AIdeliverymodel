# Maintenance Model

> ⚠️ **Experimental area.** Product Operations / Run is not yet stabilized. This is a first version to adapt.

How a product is kept healthy, current, and correct over its life.

## What maintenance covers

- **Corrective** — fixing defects and failures.
- **Adaptive** — keeping up with changing data, dependencies, and environments.
- **Evolutive** — functional evolution owned by the business.
- **AI upkeep** — reviewing prompts, narrative models, thresholds, and validation as behavior drifts.
- **Data quality upkeep** — sustaining the quality the product depends on.

## Principles

- Maintenance is planned and funded as part of the product, not treated as an unbudgeted surprise (Product over Project).
- Each maintenance responsibility has a named owner (see [ownership-model.md](ownership-model.md)).
- Maintenance work is driven by what the [observability matrix](observability-matrix.md) reveals.
- Changes update the [AI Impact Canvas](../02-ai-discovery/ai-impact-canvas.md) and the [decision log](../templates/decision-log.md).
