## Objective

By the end of this workshop we will have a rich, accurate corpus and a first-pass [AI Impact Canvas](ai-impact-canvas.md), with Run impact and business responsibilities made explicit.

> Principle: **AI produces the analyses. Humans build consensus and make decisions.**

---

## Before the workshop (preparation)

- [ ] Collect the corpus: business documents, notes, diagrams/schemas, known constraints.
- [ ] Write the business question and scope in one paragraph.
- [ ] Confirm participants, including a business owner who can accept responsibilities.
- [ ] Set up transcription / note capture.
- [ ] Remove or generalize any confidential/identifying details from shared material.

## Agenda

| Time | Segment | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Framing | State the business question and scope; agree the goal of the session |
| 0:10 | Business value & process | Capture the outcome, why it matters, and the process it touches |
| 0:30 | Users & roles | Who uses it, in what role |
| 0:40 | Data, connectors, quality | Where data comes from; integrations; quality needed and who owns it |
| 1:00 | Visualization & AI capabilities | What the business expects to see; AI behavior required and its limits |
| 1:20 | Break | — |
| 1:30 | Architecture & security | Solution shape; security/compliance obligations |
| 1:50 | **Build & Run impact** | For each choice, discuss Build effort *and* Run cost/supervision/ownership |
| 2:15 | Business responsibilities | Walk through and confirm downstream responsibilities the business accepts |
| 2:30 | Open questions & decisions | Capture unknowns, decisions required, owners, and dates |
| 2:45 | Close | Recap; next steps; who runs the specialized analyses |

*Adjust timing to your context. The Build & Run impact and Business responsibilities segments are the ones not to skip.*

## During the workshop — facilitation notes

- Capture, don't decide. The live goal is a rich corpus, not premature conclusions.
- For every requested capability, ask: *what would this cost to run and supervise?*
- Record every open question with an owner.

## After the workshop

- [ ] Complete the [Go / No-Go recommendation](discovery/14-go-no-go-recommendation.md).
- [ ] Assemble the corpus (transcript + documents + notes + diagrams + constraints).
- [ ] Run the specialized analyses using the [prompts](../prompts/).
- [ ] Consolidate into the [AI Impact Canvas](ai-impact-canvas.md) via the [orchestrator](../prompts/orchestrator.md).
- [ ] Hold human arbitration; record outcomes in the [decision log](decision-log.md).
- [ ] Complete the [ownership matrix](ownership-matrix.md) and a draft observability plan.
