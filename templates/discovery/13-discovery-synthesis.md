## Facilitator note

This synthesis is written **with** the room, not afterwards for it. Read it back before people leave: if someone disagrees, that disagreement is the most valuable output of the session.

Honest gaps stay as gaps. A synthesis full of `[to be confirmed]` with named owners is a good outcome; a synthesis full of confident guesses is not.
