## Purpose

Define precisely what the deliverable is, for whom, in what format and at what frequency — including the narrative requirements and who may edit the output.

## Facilitator questions

- What exactly must the final deliverable look like? Show me the closest thing that exists today.
- Who receives it, and what do they do with it?
- How often, and in what format?
- Can a human edit the generated output before it goes out? Who? Is that tracked?

## Guardrails

- Ask for an existing example — a real artifact beats a description.
- "Nice to have" visualizations carry Run cost too; mark their priority.
- If auditability is required, it changes the architecture — flag it to [06 Architecture Options](06-architecture-options-canvas.md).
