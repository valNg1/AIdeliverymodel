# AI-native Discovery Workbook

**Single working support for the workshop.** Copy this file, rename it for your use case, and fill it live during the session.

> **AI produces analyses. Humans build consensus and make decisions.**
> The human facilitator remains responsible for all arbitrations.

This workbook assembles the working grids of every canvas **in workshop order**. Each section links back to its full canvas, which carries the facilitator questions, detailed sub-tables and guardrails. Use the workbook to *fill*; use the canvases to *guide*.

- Agenda, facilitator guidance and preparation checklist: **[00 Workshop Readme](00-workshop-readme.md)**
- Partially filled example: **[automated-reporting-example.md](../../examples/discovery/automated-reporting-example.md)**

## Facilitator reminders

1. Do not try to fill every box — honest gaps beat confident guesses.
2. Separate facts, assumptions and unknowns.
3. Do not let the business hand every responsibility to IT.
4. Make Build **and** Run consequences visible for each request.
5. Write decisions down live.
6. No architecture validated without a human expert.
7. An AI output is never a decision.
8. Mark `[to be confirmed]`, then give it an owner and a date.
