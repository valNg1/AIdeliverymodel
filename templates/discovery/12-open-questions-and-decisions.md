## Purpose

Capture decisions **as they are made** and questions **as they surface** — with owners and dates. A decision remembered differently next week is not a decision.

### Field guidance

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Decision** | What was decided, in one sentence, unambiguously |
| **Date** | When it was made |
| **Owner** | The **named human** who made or owns the decision |
| **Options Considered** | What else was on the table — proves it was a choice |
| **Rationale** | Why this option; the reasoning future readers will need |
| **Impact** | What this changes for Build, Run, cost or scope |
| **Review Date** | When to revisit; blank means never, which is rarely right |

> Decisions to expect from this workshop: scope, in-scope data sources, AI vs. deterministic split, architecture shortlist, whether generated output may reach users unreviewed, ownership assignments, and the next step.

|---|---|
| **Question** | Specific enough that an answer would close it |

## Guardrails

- Write it down **during** the session, not afterwards from memory.
- An open question without an owner and a date is not tracked — it is forgotten.
- Deferring a decision *is* a decision: log it, with who defers and until when.
- AI-produced analyses may inform a decision; the Owner column is always a human.
