## Purpose

Understand the process as it actually happens today, define the target process, and make **human validation and approval points explicit**.

## Facilitator questions

- Walk me through the process as it happens today, step by step.
- Which steps are manual? Where are the decision points and the exceptions?
- Who validates, and who approves — today and in the target?
- What happens when something goes wrong?

## Guardrails

- Capture the process as it *is*, not as it is supposed to be.
- Exceptions are where the real cost lives — do not skip them.
- A validation point with no named human is not a validation point.
