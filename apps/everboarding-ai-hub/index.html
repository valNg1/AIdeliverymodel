AI PRODUCT ENGINEERING METHODOLOGY

Intent-based delivery

Prototype early, learn fast, stabilise the model, then build for real.

WHY THIS METHODFrom request to needThe thirteen phasesRequirement statusPrototype & reviewRequalify the modelBuild light, validateFreeze MVPMeasure & remember

Why this method

The first formulation of a requirement is a hypothesis. AI lowers the cost of producing something concrete early enough for business users to react to it.

Only what has stopped moving gets built for real.

The thirteen phases

Core rule

AI may infer, propose and classify. It never silently moves information to Confirmed. Human agreement remains explicit.

ORGANIZATIONAL DESIGN

CTO Office Factory operating model

A practical model for turning isolated AI initiatives into reusable delivery capability.

TARGET OPERATING MODEL Roles & responsibilities Factory backlog Governance cadence Industrialization gates Transition roadmap

Target operating model

The Factory is organised around four complementary responsibilities. The objective is to reduce interfaces, reuse what can be shared and keep business decisions with the business.

LayerPrimary responsibilityCore outputs
Business / ProductFrame the problem, own value, rules, priorities and acceptance.Intent, decisions, business acceptance
ImplementationPrototype, build, test and prepare industrialization.Working product, tests, handover
Factory FoundationProvide reusable services and standards.Connectors, agents, CI/CD, security, observability
Run / OperationsOperate, monitor, support and feed usage back into the product.Service health, incidents, learning signals

One intake, two backlogs

Every new demand enters through the Hub. Product-specific capabilities stay in the Product Backlog; reusable technical capabilities move into the Factory Backlog.

Build once when the capability is common. Keep product logic close to the product.

Architecture

Portability comes from separating source adapters, normalized context, business rules, agents and screens.

THE STACK, LAYER BY LAYERImplementation organizationWhy these choicesCompatibilityLooking further

The stack, layer by layer

InterfaceStandalone web UI, independent from source systems.
ApplicationDomain services and orchestration separated from rendering.
DataGit/project descriptors first; optional backend later.
IdentityDesigned for approved SSO in enterprise.
DeliveryGit as technical source of truth; versioned releases and handover packs.
TestsAutomated checks + Test Book + Human Acceptance.

One direction of dependency

Source → Adapter → Normalized context → Application services → Screens / Agents.
