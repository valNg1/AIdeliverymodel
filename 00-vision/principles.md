# Principles

Eleven structuring principles hold the AI Delivery Model together. They are not independent slogans; each one constrains and reinforces the others. Use them as decision filters: when a choice is unclear, check it against these.

---

### 1. Business First

Start from business value, not technology. Every product, feature, and technical decision must trace back to a business outcome. If a choice cannot be connected to value, it is a candidate for removal.

**In practice:** the AI Impact Canvas opens with Business Value; nothing downstream is justified without it.

---

### 2. Single Product Ownership

Each product has one accountable owner, end to end — from need to Run. Shared ownership is no ownership. The owner is responsible for value, trade-offs, and the product's health in production.

**In practice:** the [ownership model](../05-product-operations/ownership-model.md) names a single accountable owner per product.

---

### 3. Human + AI Delivery Team

AI is a member of the delivery team, working in defined roles with explicit inputs, outputs, and boundaries — not an opaque tool bolted on. The team is designed as humans *and* AI together.

**In practice:** the [human-AI team model](../01-operating-model/human-ai-team-model.md) and the [agent prompts](../prompts/) define those roles.

---

### 4. Knowledge First

Knowledge is a first-class asset. Capture it, structure it, and make it reusable — decisions, patterns, domain rules, and lessons. The organization should get smarter with every delivery.

**In practice:** see [knowledge-first](../03-delivery/knowledge-first.md) and the [learning system](../06-learning-system/).

---

### 5. Reference-Driven Delivery

Build from proven references, patterns, and examples rather than from a blank page. References reduce risk, speed delivery, and encode what already works.

**In practice:** see [reference-driven-delivery](../03-delivery/reference-driven-delivery.md).

---

### 6. Continuous Design

Design is ongoing, not a single upfront phase. As understanding grows and the product runs, the design keeps adapting. Discovery does not "finish" and hand off a frozen spec.

**In practice:** see [continuous-design](../03-delivery/continuous-design.md).

---

### 7. Reusable Building Blocks

Assemble products from shared, reusable components — technical and knowledge-based. Reuse lowers cost, raises consistency, and compounds over time.

**In practice:** see [reusable-assets](../03-delivery/reusable-assets.md).

---

### 8. Human Validation

Humans validate, arbitrate, and decide. AI-produced analyses are inputs to human judgment, never a substitute for it. Validation is a gate, not a rubber stamp.

**In practice:** see [human-validation](../03-delivery/human-validation.md).

---

### 9. Product over Project

Fund and manage durable products, not temporary projects. A project ends and disperses accountability; a product persists, with an owner and a Run plan.

**In practice:** see [portfolio governance](../04-platform-and-governance/portfolio-governance.md).

---

### 10. Continuous Learning

Every delivery feeds the learning system: retrospectives, updated references, and captured knowledge. Learning is designed in, not hoped for.

**In practice:** see [continuous-learning](../06-learning-system/continuous-learning.md).

---

### 11. Delivery as an Organizational Capability

Treat the organization's ability to deliver as a strategic asset to grow deliberately — measured, invested in, and improved. This is the capability the "From Buy to Make" thesis depends on.

**In practice:** see [from-buy-to-make](from-buy-to-make.md) and the [maturity model](../06-learning-system/maturity-model.md).

---

## Using the principles together

- **Business First** and **Product over Project** decide *what* you build and fund.
- **Single Product Ownership** and **Human Validation** decide *who* is accountable.
- **Human + AI Delivery Team**, **Reference-Driven Delivery**, and **Reusable Building Blocks** decide *how* you build.
- **Knowledge First**, **Continuous Design**, and **Continuous Learning** decide how you *improve*.
- **Delivery as an Organizational Capability** is the outcome all the others produce.

See the [manifesto](manifesto.md) for the stance behind these principles.
