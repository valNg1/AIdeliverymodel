# Manifesto

We believe the way organizations deliver digital products is changing, and that the change is structural, not cosmetic.

## What we believe

**Software is no longer only something you buy.**
For two decades the rational default was to buy software and outsource its integration. That default was shaped by the high cost of producing software. As that cost falls, buying everything strategic is no longer obviously right. Organizations can build their strategic products again — and should, where those products are a source of differentiation.

**The strategic asset is the capability, not the artifact.**
A single product is an outcome. The ability to produce, operate, maintain, and evolve products — repeatably — is the asset. We optimize for that capability.

**Business comes first.**
Technology choices exist to serve business value. We start from the need and the value, and we let those pull the rest.

**AI is a team member, not magic.**
AI belongs on the delivery team in defined roles, with clear inputs, outputs, and boundaries. It accelerates analysis and production. It does not absolve anyone of judgment.

**Humans decide.**
AI produces analyses. Humans build consensus, arbitrate trade-offs, validate results, and make decisions. Accountability stays with people.

**Discovery must reveal the whole cost of a choice.**
A need is cheap to state and expensive to run. From Discovery onward, we connect the need to its Build *and* Run consequences — data, architecture, security, cost, supervision, and ownership — so choices are made with their real weight visible.

**The business owns more than the need.**
Expressing a need is not the end of business responsibility; it is the beginning. Business owners accept responsibility for business rules, data quality, validation, thresholds, prompts, narrative models, supervision, and functional evolution.

**Knowledge and reuse compound.**
Every delivery should leave the organization more capable: more captured knowledge, more reusable building blocks, more proven references. We build from what exists and we feed what comes next.

**Products, not projects.**
We fund and manage durable products with owners, not temporary projects that end and leave no one accountable.

**We stay honest about maturity.**
Where our practices are not yet stable — today, notably the Run — we say so, and we treat those areas as experiments to learn from, not as settled doctrine.

## What we reject

- Treating AI as an unaccountable oracle.
- Delivering products that nobody planned to operate.
- Discovery that captures a wish list but hides the downstream cost.
- One-off projects that leave no reusable knowledge behind.
- Governance so slow that it pushes people into shadow IT.

## The one line to remember

> **AI produces the analyses. Humans build consensus and make decisions.**
