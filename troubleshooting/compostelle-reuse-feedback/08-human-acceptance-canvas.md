## Capability
Reuse — qualitative feedback after a learner submits a sentence.

## 1. What changed?

- After submitting a sentence, the learner now sees a clear **assessment** ("correct" / "good attempt — understandable" / "use the target expression").
- A **plain corrected version** of the sentence is shown (not just coloured diff highlights).
- The **main correction natures** are listed (e.g. grammar, spelling).
- A **Try again** button lets the learner make a second attempt without leaving the screen.
- The **score is no longer the main message** — it is secondary.

*(Automated tests already prove the structure, the "never invent an error on a correct sentence" rule, and the blank-input / empty-corrections edges. Do not re-test those below — validate product judgement only.)*
