# 02 — Capability Intent

> Applied case — Compostelle. Applies [Capability Intent](../../03-delivery/intent-based-development.md#two-levels-of-intent).

The durable behaviour the product owes the learner — technology-free.

## Capability Intent

> When a learner attempts to reuse a learned expression in a sentence, the product should help them understand the **quality of their production** and **how to improve it**.

## Expected learner outcome

After submitting a sentence, the learner should be able to:

1. understand whether the sentence is acceptable;
2. see a minimally corrected version;
3. understand the most important correction;
4. see one more idiomatic alternative;
5. optionally try again.

## Acceptance signal

The learner can make a **better second attempt without external help**.

## Not in scope here

No implementation technology is named — not LanguageTool, not an LLM, not a provider. Capability Intent describes **expected behaviour**, not the technical solution. Engine choice is settled later, against the [acceptance criteria](03-acceptance-criteria.md) and the [gap analysis](04-gap-analysis.md).
