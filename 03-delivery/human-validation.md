# Human Validation

**Principle:** humans validate, arbitrate, and decide. AI-produced analyses are inputs to human judgment, never a substitute for it.

## Why

Accountability cannot be delegated to a model. Validation is the gate where a named human confirms that outputs, data quality, and behavior meet the bar — and accepts responsibility for what goes live.

> **AI produces the analyses. Humans build consensus and make decisions.**

## What humans validate

- **Outputs** — results are correct and fit for purpose.
- **Data quality** — inputs meet the required quality.
- **Business rules** — the product enforces the intended rules.
- **Thresholds** — alerts and limits are set correctly.
- **Prompts and narrative models** — AI behavior is appropriate and bounded.

## How

- Validation is a defined gate in the [lifecycle](../01-operating-model/lifecycle.md), not an afterthought.
- The validator is a named owner (see [ownership matrix](../templates/ownership-matrix.md)).
- Validation decisions and residual risks are recorded in the [decision log](../templates/decision-log.md).

## Guardrail

A validation gate that always passes is not a gate. Validators must have the authority and the information to say no.
