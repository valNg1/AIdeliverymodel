# Reference-Driven Delivery

**Principle:** build from proven references, patterns, and examples rather than from a blank page.

## Why

References reduce risk, speed delivery, and encode what already works. They also make AI-assisted delivery more reliable: given a good reference, an AI role produces more consistent, review-ready output.

## What counts as a reference

- Proven architecture and integration patterns.
- Prior AI Impact Canvases for similar needs.
- Validated prompts and narrative models.
- Reusable building blocks with known behavior.

## How

- Start Design by searching the [reference model](../06-learning-system/reference-model.md) for close matches.
- Prefer adapting a reference over inventing from scratch.
- When you deviate, record why in the [decision log](../templates/decision-log.md).
- Feed new proven work back as a reference (see [continuous learning](../06-learning-system/continuous-learning.md)).
