# Knowledge First

**Principle:** knowledge is a first-class asset. Capture it, structure it, and make it reusable, so the organization gets smarter with every delivery.

## Why

In the standard model, knowledge often left with the project or the external integrator. The AI Delivery Model keeps it. Captured knowledge is what makes [reference-driven delivery](reference-driven-delivery.md) and [reusable building blocks](reusable-assets.md) possible, and it is the raw material of the [learning system](../06-learning-system/).

## What to capture

- **Decisions and their rationale** — see the [decision log](../templates/decision-log.md).
- **Domain rules and constraints** — business rules, data quality expectations, thresholds.
- **Patterns and references** — what worked, what to reuse.
- **Lessons** — from retrospectives and from the Run.

## How

- Capture as you go, not at the end.
- Keep knowledge close to where it is used and linked from the [AI Impact Canvas](../02-ai-discovery/ai-impact-canvas.md).
- Feed captured knowledge into the [reference model](../06-learning-system/reference-model.md).
